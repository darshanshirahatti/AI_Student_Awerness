New AI Technologies

Generative AI

Creates new text, images, or music. Example: ChatGPT, Midjourney.

Computer Vision

Helps computers "see" images and video. Example: Face ID, Self-driving cars.

NLP

Natural Language Processing helps computers understand human speech.

Speech AI

Recognizes spoken words or generates speech. Examples include captions and voice navigation.

AI Robotics

Combines AI with machines that sense their surroundings and choose actions, such as warehouse robots.
