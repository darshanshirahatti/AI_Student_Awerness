What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that enables computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

How AI Works

Learning from Data

AI looks at millions of examples to learn how things work.

Pattern Recognition

It spots patterns, like recognizing a cat in a photo.

Predictions

It uses patterns to guess what might happen next.

Language Understanding

It learns to read, write, and speak human languages.

Image Recognition

It can "see" and understand what is in a picture.

Content Generation

It can create new text, images, or music based on what it learned.
