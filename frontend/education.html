AI in Education

Use AI as a study partner that helps you understand, practice, and explore.

Explain a hard topic

Ask for a simpler explanation, an example, or a step-by-step walkthrough. Check important details against your class materials.

Practice questions

Ask for practice questions one at a time. Try answering before you ask for hints or explanations.

Language support

Use Language AI to explore translations and learn in English, Kannada, Hindi, or Marathi.

Improve a draft

Request feedback on clarity or grammar, then decide which suggestions fit your meaning and voice.

Explore project ideas

Use AI to brainstorm a starting point, then research the topic and make the project your own.

Accessibility

Speech input, text-to-speech, and summaries can offer different ways to access learning materials.

AI should support learning, not replace your own thinking.

🤖 AI Awareness for School Students

Use AI to learn better, think better, and communicate better — not to stop thinking.
