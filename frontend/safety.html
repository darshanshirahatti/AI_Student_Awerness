AI Safety & Responsible Use

AI Safety Center

Data Privacy

Never share passwords, OTPs, or bank details with an AI chatbot. They can store your chats!

Verification

Don't blindly believe AI answers. Verify important information using trusted sources like textbooks.

Originality

Use AI for ideas and grammar, but do not submit AI-generated homework as your own work.

Ask for Help

Ask a teacher or parent if something feels unsafe, inappropriate, or weird.
