Benefits & Risks of AI

BENEFITS

  • Saves time and improves productivity
  • Provides quick explanations for studying
  • Automates repetitive tasks
  • Improves accessibility for disabled users

RISKS

  • Incorrect information (AI Hallucinations)
  • Privacy risks (sharing personal data)
  • Over-dependence (stopping your own thinking)
  • Fake images, videos, and misleading news
