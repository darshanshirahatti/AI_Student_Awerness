What is NLP? 🗣️

Natural Language Processing (NLP) explained for students.

How Computers Understand Us

Computers naturally only understand numbers (0s and 1s). But humans speak in languages like English, Kannada, Hindi, and Marathi!

Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a type of Artificial Intelligence that teaches computers how to read, understand, and even speak human languages.

Examples of NLP around you:

  • 🎙️ Voice Assistants (Alexa, Siri)
  • 🌐 Google Translate
  • ✍️ Grammarly (Spell Check)
  • 🤖 Chatbots (ChatGPT)
