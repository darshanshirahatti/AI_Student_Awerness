AI & Communication

💬 How AI helps communication

AI tools like grammar checkers, translators, and smart replies help people communicate faster and more effectively across different languages.

  • Real-time language translation
  • Grammar and spell checking (like Grammarly)
  • Voice-to-Text and Text-to-Speech
  • Summarizing long emails

Important Warning

"Always check AI-generated messages before sharing them."

AI can make mistakes or sound unnatural. Always read and edit the response to make it your own.
